Time Space Compression

What is Time Space Compression

Time space compression is the geographic concept that describe how increased globalisation can occur through enhancements in communication technology creating a world that feels increasingly interconnected.

Positive Contributions to Society

  • reduces time needed to share ideas
  • allows for people to share expieriences of events first hand
  • creates a greater sense of global understanding as people are able to share cultural ideas and norms without travelling

Reducing time constraints to sharing ideas

With the development of technology people across the globe are able to communicate in real time. Communicating without time constraints allows for new ideas to be collaborated on
